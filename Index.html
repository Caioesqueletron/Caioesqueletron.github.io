A little about me

I'm a Computer Engineering graduate and full stack software engineer, with a strong focus on backend development and on scalable, high-performance systems built with Java and Spring Boot. On the frontend I work with React. I apply clean code, SOLID principles and software architecture patterns to deliver robust, maintainable solutions.

At Alelo, I worked on the Portal Minha Empresa (PME) team, building backend solutions aimed at cost reduction, higher transaction volumes and a larger average ticket size. Before that, on the chatbot team, I contributed to performance and scalability by maintaining and refactoring critical microservices.

One of the most impactful projects I led was the IP Segregation initiative: I redesigned order flows and technically led the segregation of legal processes under a new product identifier, delivering the legal process segregation feature in 2 weeks and in compliance with Central Bank regulations.

My last project at CI&T was at AB InBev, where I worked on a large data synchronization gateway, with Kafka, RabbitMQ, Redis and resilience patterns, and helped create the contracts for onboarding new partners.
